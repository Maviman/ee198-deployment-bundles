# The dead zone: cars past the tape and cars the cameras lose (2026-11-19)

Branch `claude/edge-recovery`, cut from `claude/hold-radius` (what both Orins ran on 2026-11-18).
Follows [2026-11-18-tag-dropouts.md](2026-11-18-tag-dropouts.md). **To change or debug it, read
[2026-11-19-dead-zone-iteration.md](2026-11-19-dead-zone-iteration.md)**: the code map, the run-log
reader, symptom-to-knob table, invariants and known weak points. Nothing here was run on the
real cars: it was written and tested on the dev PC (unit tests and a simple simulated car). Read
"Check on the rig" before trusting it.

## The problem

A car that passed the tape, or left the cameras' view, ended the run:

- the camera code rejected any pose more than 0.15 m past the tape;
- perception sent NOTHING while any vehicle was missing, so the controller stalled and E-stopped;
- the dashboard disarmed the moment a car's centre touched the tape.

So nothing could bring a car back, although the 3-car models were trained with exactly that
behaviour (`rc16/hive/recovery.py`: past the tape, drive back in; unseen, brake and replay the
last second of commands backwards). The deployment never told them a car was unseen.

## What happens now

Everything the cameras do not cover is treated as a dead zone the car crosses blind.

| the car is | what it is sent |
|---|---|
| seen, inside the tape | the model's command. One-car models also get an **edge brake**: too fast to stop before the tape, the throttle becomes a brake; a throttle toward the tape is capped at the speed it could still stop from |
| seen, past the tape | brake, then drive toward the centre at about 0.4 m/s until 0.2 m inside (3-car models: 0.3 m, their own rule) |
| not seen | brake against its last measured motion, then **replay the commands it was sent while it was seen, backwards**: newest first, the same steering, a slow throttle the other way. It retraces its own track to where the cameras last saw it |
| not seen for 4.5 s (3-car models: 3 s), or 12 s in recovery | zero command; the dashboard ends the run with "car N lost" |
| the evader is not seen | every car stands still, armed, until it is back (a car too fast to stop before the tape is still braked) |
| the model has no car numbers in its manifest (older models), or `off` | an unseen car is only stopped, and the run still ends at the tape |

The commands are remembered on the controller, which sent every one of them. **The car firmware
is unchanged**; its 300 ms dead-man still stops the car if the radio drops.

Silence still means stop: a dead camera or a crashed vision process sends nothing, and the
controller E-stops after 0.3 s as before.

## Where it lives

- `portable_orin_perception/.../core/frame_builder.py`: a lost vehicle is sent as
  `{"x": null, ..., "lost_s": 0.62, "last": {...}}` instead of silencing the stream. A held pose
  carries its own capture time. The evader is held 1.0 s (a car parked beside it hides its tag).
  An older controller cannot parse the nulls and stalls, so a version mismatch fails safe.
- `portable_orin_perception/.../core/localizer.py`: cars are tracked 0.45 m past the tape (was
  0.15 m); the jump gate allows 3.5 m/s (was 2.0, under the car's 2.7 m/s cap: a fast car
  reappearing after a short miss was being rejected).
- `portable_n1_controller/pc_controller/deadzone.py`: the table above. `EdgeRecovery` is a
  one-car port of the 3-car recovery, for models that have none.
- `pc_controller/runtimes/rc16_hive_v1.py`: `step(..., seen=)`. The 3-car runtime now gets the
  seen flags and runs its own trained recovery and limiter on them.
- `pc_controller/realtime.py`, `loops.py`, `pose_stream.py`: the plumbing. A lost car is no
  longer a stall.
- Each pose frame now carries the taped square the cameras were calibrated on (`"arena"`). For
  one-car models the controller takes "the tape" from it, so a 6 ft square under an 8 ft model is
  handled (it logs the difference). The 3-car runtime keeps the 8 ft arena it was trained on.
- `deploy/hub.py`, `deploy/dashboard.html`: the run no longer ends at the tape. A lost car is
  drawn faded where it was last seen, labelled `OUT OF VIEW: BACKING UP`, `DRIVING BACK IN` or
  `LOST`.

## Settings (control Orin)

`run_controller.py --recovery WORDS`, or one line in `~/.arena/recovery`, or `ARENA_RECOVERY`:

| word | meaning |
|---|---|
| `on` (default) / `off` | `off`: a lost car is only stopped, never driven blind |
| `pwm` (default) / `binary` | the car firmware's `drive` setting. **`binary`: the slow recovery throttle is sent as on/off pulses.** In `drive binary` a throttle under 1/3 does nothing, and the recovery asks for 0.19 to 0.30 |
| `min=0.25` | the smallest throttle that moves the car (a PWM car that does not turn at a low duty) |
| `guard` (default) / `noguard` | the edge brake of one-car models |

Vision: `run_vision.py --bounds-margin-m 0.45`, `--evader-hold-max-age-s 1.0`,
`--no-report-lost` (the old silent behaviour). `arena up` needs no change: the defaults are on.

Optional, off by default: `vehicle_tag_height_m` in `config/marker_map.yaml` corrects each
camera's poses for the tag riding on the roof (a tag 9 cm up reads up to 20 cm further from the
camera than the car is, at the far tape). It needs the lens matrix `arena scan` measures. The
vision log prints where it places each camera; check that against the rig before believing it.

## Check on the rig, in this order

1. `cd portable_n1_controller && python3 -m pytest tests -q` and the same in
   `portable_orin_perception`. `tests/test_realtime.py` and `tests/test_serial_link.py` could
   NOT be run on the dev PC (they need Linux ptys); run them here first.
2. `arena up`, DISARMED: push a car by hand over the tape and out of view. The dashboard should
   keep running (no "pose feed lost"), draw the car faded, and show `LOST` after 4.5 s.
3. Which drive mode is the car in? Serial `drive`. The MD Elite models expect `drive pwm` with
   `limit` set so a full command reads 2.7 m/s. In `drive binary`, put `binary` in
   `~/.arena/recovery`, or the blind recovery sends throttles the car ignores.
4. Find the smallest throttle that moves the car (dashboard MANUAL DRIVE, power slider). If it is
   above 0.19, put `min=<that>` in `~/.arena/recovery`.
5. Armed, with room around the arena: start the car near an edge facing out. Expect a short
   excursion, a brake, a slow reverse back into view, then the chase resumes.

## What the simulation says, and what it cannot say

A simple model of the car (bicycle steering, speed following the throttle with a 0.35 s lag,
4.8 m/s^2 brake) under cameras that see 0.10 m past the tape, with a model that holds its
throttle straight through the tape:

- Edge brake on: a car with room to stop was never more than 0.15 m past the tape and never
  left view for more than 0.3 s, with up to 0.1 s of command delay.
- Edge brake off (blind recovery alone): the car was 0.9 to 1.7 m out before it stopped. It came
  back into view in 107 of 108 runs (throttle 0.3 to 1.0, forward and reverse, straight and
  curved, 0 to 0.2 s delay). The one failure: full throttle, 0.2 s delay, 1.7 m out.
- Before the freshness fix, perception's 0.25 s pose hold let the model drive on a frozen pose:
  a car leaving at 2 m/s was 0.8 m out before anything braked. The controller now treats a
  pose older than 0.12 s as unseen.

The real car is not this model. Its braking, its slowest speed and how far it rolls are not
measured here; the numbers above are behaviour checks, not predictions of distance.
